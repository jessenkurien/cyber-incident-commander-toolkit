# Incident Communications Matrix

| Audience | Purpose | Owner | Cadence / trigger | Channel | Approver | Minimum content |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Technical responders | Coordinate actions and evidence | Operations Lead | Continuous | Approved response channel | Incident Commander | Facts, actions, owners, blockers |
| Incident command | Set priorities and resolve tradeoffs | Incident Commander | 30–60 min | Command bridge | Incident Commander | Impact, progress, decisions, forward risk |
| Executive leadership | Enable risk and business decisions | Executive Liaison | 30–60 min or material change | Executive bridge / written brief | Incident Commander | Business impact, confidence, decisions, next milestone |
| Employees | Provide safe, actionable direction | Internal Communications | Material change | Approved internal channel | Communications + Legal | What to do, what not to do, where to report |
| Customers / partners | Maintain trust and meet commitments | Customer Communications | Contractual or impact trigger | Approved customer channel | Legal + Communications | Validated impact, action taken, customer guidance |
| Regulators / authorities | Meet applicable obligations | Legal / Privacy | Legal trigger | Approved formal channel | Legal | Required facts and timing only |
| Media / public | Maintain accurate public record | External Communications | Approved trigger | Authorized spokesperson | Legal + Executive Sponsor | Consistent approved statement |

## Message quality check

- Is the audience and desired action clear?
- Are facts, hypotheses, and unknowns separated?
- Is confidence stated without false precision?
- Does the message avoid exposing investigative details or sensitive evidence?
- Are legal, privacy, contractual, and regulatory inputs complete?
- Is the next update time or trigger explicit?
